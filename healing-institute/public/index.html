Why we exist

Finding the right specialist shouldn’t be this hard.

Most therapists are trained for general mental health work. If your needs go beyond that, finding someone with truly specialized skills can feel almost impossible.

The Healing Institute was created to close that gap: a practice where people with specific, complex needs can find the absolute best care possible, using neuroscience, cutting-edge techniques and solid evidence-based approaches.

Meet your therapist

Brian J. Robertson

MSW, LCSW, EMDR-C · “Most people know me as Rob.”

EMDRIA Certified ISSTD Member Complex trauma specialist

Rob works solely with adults whose histories involve complex trauma, including C-PTSD, borderline personality disorder, eating disorders and dissociative disorders. He also has experience supporting survivors of organized and extreme abuse.

His approach emphasizes compassion, collaboration and evidence-based trauma treatment, always tailored to you.
