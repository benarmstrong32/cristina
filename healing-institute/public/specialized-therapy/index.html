Our approach

Evidence-based, trauma-informed, and tailored to you.

There is no one-size-fits-all path. Depending on your needs, therapy may draw on a combination of approaches designed to build stability first and then gently resolve what’s been stuck.

  • EMDR
  • Brainspotting
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)
  • Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)
  • Internal Family Systems (IFS)
  • Ego State Therapy
  • Somatic therapy
  • Clinical hypnosis
  • ACT
