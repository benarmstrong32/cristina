Our story

Born from a gap in care

The Healing Institute, PLLC was founded by psychotherapist and licensed clinical social worker Brian J. Robertson, MSW, LCSW. Working in the mental healthcare field, he found that people who needed treatment often had a hard time finding subject-matter experts for specific concerns.

Most people were used to finding therapists for general mental health work. But if their needs were specialized, finding an experienced psychotherapist with those skills was almost impossible.

Brian saw this gap in specialized providers and decided to build a practice where people with specific needs could find the absolute best care possible. Thus, the Healing Institute was born.

Our mission
