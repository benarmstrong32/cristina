Work with Rob

Brian J. Robertson

MSW, LCSW, EMDR-C

EMDRIA-certified EMDR therapist Member, ISSTD

My name is Brian Robertson, though most people know me as Rob. I am an EMDRIA-certified EMDR therapist who specializes in working with adults who have experienced complex trauma. Because these conditions require specialized care, I solely treat individuals whose histories involve complex trauma. My clinical focus includes conditions commonly associated with complex trauma, including CPTSD, borderline personality disorder, eating disorders, and dissociative disorders.

In addition, I have experience supporting survivors of organized abuse, including survivors of child exploitation, sex trafficking, and other forms of coordinated abuse. My work also includes supporting individuals who report experiences of ritual abuse and coercive control within cult environments. I am a member of the International Society for the Study of Trauma and Dissociation and participate in the organization’s Special Interest Group for the Treatment of Organized and Extreme Abuse.

How I work

My therapeutic approach emphasizes compassion, collaboration, and the use of evidence-based trauma treatments. I also integrate additional trauma-informed approaches depending on each individual’s needs. Some of the primary modalities I use:

  • EMDR therapy
  • Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)
  • Ego State Therapy
  • Internal Family Systems (IFS)
  • Clinical hypnosis
  • Somatic therapy
  • Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)

The mind-body connection

Complex trauma can also affect physical health and nervous system functioning. I often help individuals who experience conditions that can be caused or worsened by trauma, including:

  • Eating disorders
  • Psychogenic non-epileptic seizures (PNES)
  • POTS
  • Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS)
  • Fibromyalgia
  • Functional Neurological Disorder

Approaches that address the mind-body connection, such as somatic therapy and clinical hypnosis, can be particularly helpful for these concerns.

I believe that people are not defined by what has happened to them. Healing from trauma involves understanding past experiences while also developing new ways of relating to oneself and the world.

My approach combines evidence-based trauma therapy with a holistic, person-centered perspective. Therapy is a collaborative process, and I work closely with each client to help them move toward the life they want to live.

If you are looking for a therapist who specializes in complex trauma, dissociation, or trauma-related conditions, I invite you to reach out. Together we can begin the process of healing and building a path toward greater stability, resilience, and emotional well-being.
