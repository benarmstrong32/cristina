FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

What does “unlimited” really mean?

You can add as many video requests to your queue as you like. We work on one request at a time, and as soon as one is delivered we start the next. Want more moving at once? Add-on requests are $3,000 per request.

How fast will I get my videos?

Our turnaround time is 7–10 days.

Do you film, or just edit?

Both. Unlimited video production is included, with one production day reserved at a time. We film in our Raleigh studio or on location anywhere in the USA; you just cover billable expenses. Need more shoot time? Add-on production days are $3,000 per day.

What else is included?

Unlimited creative strategy, scriptwriting and storyboarding, editing, revisions and stock footage, for as many brands as you need.

Which plan should I choose?

All three plans include the same unlimited membership. The longer the plan, the bigger the discount: $1,500 off the 3 month plan, $3,000 off the 6 month plan and $6,000 off the 12 month plan.

Is there a long-term commitment?

No. Unlimited is flexible with no long-term commitment. Pick the plan length that fits and decide what's next when it ends.

Is Unlimited right for my business?

If you need a steady stream of video for ads, social, recruiting, training or your brand, it's built for you. Not sure? Book a free strategy session and we'll tell you honestly whether Unlimited or a single project is the better fit.
