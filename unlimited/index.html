FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

What does “unlimited” really mean?

You can submit as many video requests as you like. We work through your queue in priority order, so you always have videos in production and new ones landing regularly.

How fast will I get my videos?

Most short-form edits are delivered within a few business days. Bigger pieces are scheduled with you up front so you always know what's coming and when.

Do you film, or just edit?

Unlimited covers editing, graphics and strategy using your footage and our libraries. Need us on location? On-site shoot days with our Raleigh production crew can be added anytime.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Unlimited is month-to-month, and you can pause or cancel whenever you need to.

Who owns the finished videos?

You do. Every finished video is yours to use however and wherever you like.

Is Unlimited right for my business?

If you need a steady stream of video for social, ads, sales or internal comms, it's built for you. Not sure? Book a call and we'll tell you honestly whether Unlimited or a single project is the better fit.
