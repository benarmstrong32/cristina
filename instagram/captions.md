## 04-phone.png
**Caption:**
Worried about your phone? Trust that feeling. 📱

If someone always seems to know where you've been or what you've said, technology may be part of it.

• Use a safer device, like a library computer or a trusted friend's phone, to look for help
• Think before deleting apps. Removing a tracker suddenly can alert the other person
• Private browsing can't hide what you do from monitoring software

More careful steps at atherpace.org.

This account shares information and isn't a crisis service. For a trained advocate 24/7: call 800-799-7233 or text START to 88788.

## 06-worried-about-someone.png
**Caption:**
Worried about someone you love? 💬

Try: "I'm worried about you. I'm here whenever you want to talk."

• Listen, and believe her
• Respect her choices and timing
• Offer practical help, like a ride, a phone, or a place to keep a bag
• Don't confront the person hurting her. It can make things more dangerous

Advocates support friends and family too: 800-799-7233.
More at atherpace.org.

Send this to someone who might need it.

#supportsurvivors #friendsandfamily #domesticviolenceawareness #healthyrelationships
